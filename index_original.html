Caring for Your Woolen Crafts

Our creations are crafted to last years. Here is how to keep them looking fresh and vibrant:

🌸 Forever Flowers

No water needed! Simply use a soft dry brush or a gentle cool hairdryer blow to dust off petals once a month.

👜 Crochet Totes & Bags

Spot clean with mild soap and cold water. Lay flat on a clean towel to dry naturally to preserve shape.

🧶 Sweaters & Cardigans

Hand wash gently in lukewarm water with wool detergent. Never wring or tumble dry; dry flat in shade.
